pub const fn pan_gains(volume: u16, pan: u8) -> (u32, u32)
Expand description

Compute the exact left/right mixer gains (vol_l, vol_r) used by the retail Thumb voice wrapper.

The wrapper computes, with 32-bit wrapping multiplication followed by a << 16 >> 23 logical-shift pair (equivalent to masking to 16 bits and shifting right by 7):

vol_l = (((127 - pan) * volume) & 0xFFFF) >> 7
vol_r = ((pan * volume) & 0xFFFF) >> 7

where volume is the 16-bit envelope/master volume and pan is the 8-bit pan byte (0 = hard left, 127 = hard right in normal use).
