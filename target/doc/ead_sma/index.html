§ead-sma-rs

Pure Rust, zero-dependency parser, software synthesizer, and byte-exact libmgba ARM golden-tested emulator for the Nintendo EAD / SRD custom DirectSound sound engine used by the Super Mario Advance series on the Game Boy Advance.

A full-corpus audit of all 3,377 GBA ROMs shows this engine is not Nintendo’s MusicPlayer2000 (M4A / Sappy): Super Mario Advance 2–4 contain no M4A SoundMainRAM mixer at all. Instead they share a proprietary ARM7TDMI software mixer (copied from ROM into IWRAM at boot) with three kernels — a nearest-neighbour voice mixer, an attenuating buffer combine, and a saturating i16 -> i8 DirectSound DMA converter — across 2 distinct revisions (EadSmaVersion::Sma23Shift8 and EadSmaVersion::Sma4Mla, 35 ROM images in total including regional, revision, Virtual Console, and China-market releases). The same engine also powers the GBA port of The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past & Four Swords and the Mario Bros. classic modes embedded in the Super Mario Advance carts. By contrast, Super Mario Advance (the first game), Mario Kart: Super Circuit, and the F-Zero GBA titles use M4A and are out of scope here.

§Features

  • Zero external dependencies ([dependencies] is empty).
  • #![no_std] compatible with default = ["std"], std = ["alloc"], alloc = [].
  • #![deny(unsafe_code)]: 100% safe Rust.
  • Dual synthesis pipelines:
    • AccuracySynth: Bit-exact emulation of both ARM7TDMI IWRAM mixer revisions (Q8 nearest-neighbour phase, per-revision volume scaling, loop wrap, attenuating combine, and saturating DMA8 conversion).
    • StudioSynth: 48,000 Hz f32 high-fidelity renderer with a compile-time Lanczos-3 polyphase LUT (plus general Lanczos-a fallback) and equal-power stereo panning.
